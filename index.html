How It Works

From banana stem to your kitchen — a journey of transformation.

1

Source

We partner with local farmers and FPOs to collect banana pseudostem — an underutilized agricultural by-product.

2

Extract

The stems are shredded and processed to extract high-quality dietary fibre through food-grade methods.

3

Transform

Raw fibre is transformed into our range of products — powders, flours, mixes, and more.

4

Deliver

Packaged with care and delivered to your doorstep. Farm-fresh nutrition, zero waste.
