Our Story

We are not simply selling banana-stem powder. We are transforming an underutilized agricultural resource into nutrition, farmer value and a scalable agri-food business.

"केले का तना, अब कचरा नहीं — पोषण और कमाई का नया ज़रिया!"

— STEMBITE Foods Team

The Problem

Banana pseudostem is an underutilized by-product after harvest. Farmers receive limited value from the residue. Millions of tonnes of this fibre-rich material go to waste every year.

Meanwhile, consumers are increasingly seeking convenient, fibre-rich food ingredients. The demand is there — the supply chain wasn't.

Our Journey

The Idea

Identifying the Gap

Research revealed meaningful dietary fibre content in banana pseudostem. Existing products validated the concept, but the market was fragmented.

Foundation

STEMBITE Foods Born

We established STEMBITE Foods with a clear mission: transform agricultural waste into everyday nutrition while creating value for farmers.

Product Development

Building the Portfolio

From initial R&D to our first products: Fibre+ powder and Flour+ blend. Food-grade processing, FSSAI certification, and quality testing protocols.

Looking Ahead

Scaling the Vision

Year 1: Validate. Year 2: Expand distribution. Year 3: Scale B2B. Projected Year-3 revenue: ₹1.14 Cr. Waste → Nutrition → Income → Growth.
