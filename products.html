Frequently Asked Questions

What is banana stem fibre?+
Banana pseudostem is the trunk-like structure of the banana plant. It's rich in dietary fibre and is typically discarded after harvest. STEMBITE transforms this agricultural waste into food-grade fibre products.
Are your products FSSAI certified?+
Yes, all STEMBITE products are FSSAI certified and comply with all food safety standards in India.
How do I use STEMBITE Fibre+?+
Mix one scoop (5g) into water, juice, smoothies, or sprinkle on food. It's tasteless and dissolves easily.
Do you offer bulk pricing for businesses?+
Yes! STEMBITE Pro is designed for B2B customers. Contact us for bulk pricing and custom requirements.
Where do you source your banana stems?+
We partner directly with local farmers and FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations) across India to collect banana pseudostem that would otherwise go to waste.
