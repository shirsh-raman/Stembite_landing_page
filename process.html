How It Works

From Stem to Superfood — the journey of transformation.

1

Collection

We partner with farmers and FPOs to collect banana pseudostem after harvest. What was once discarded becomes our raw material.

2

Sorting

Stems are sorted by quality and freshness. Only the best pseudostems make it to processing.

3

Shredding

The sorted stems are finely shredded to prepare for fibre extraction.

4

Drying

Shredded material is dried using controlled methods to preserve nutritional value.

5

Grinding

Dried fibre is ground into fine powder with consistent particle size.

6

Sieving

Multiple sieving stages ensure uniform texture and remove any impurities.

7

Testing

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality testing. FSSAI standards, fibre content, contamination checks.

8

Packaging

Food-grade packaging preserves freshness. Ready for your kitchen or factory.

Quality Assurance

Raw Material Checks

Every batch of banana pseudostem is inspected for quality, freshness, and contamination before processing begins.

Batch Testing

Fibre content analysis, microbial testing, and nutritional verification on every production batch.

Food-Grade Packaging

All products are packaged in food-grade materials with proper sealing to maintain freshness and safety.

FSSAI Compliant

All STEMBITE products comply with Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulations. We follow applicable licensing, standards, labelling, and claims requirements.

No unsupported health claims. Only substantiated and legally permitted nutrition/health claims.
